## 3. Question & Scene Engine Components

* **`src/components/question.tsx` (`Question`):**
  * Renders `mcq`, `numeric`, `text`, `teachback`, `worked_example`, and `visual` items, plus pre-question `TeachingPanel` when not in `aidless` mode.
  * Resets per-item state (`picked`, `typed`, `teach`, `receipt`, `hints`, `hintState`, `example`, `revealed`, `question`, `reply`, `error`, and `roving.setActiveIndex(0)`) synchronously during render via `shownKey = "${item.id}|${item.hint_count ?? 0}"` while restoring in-progress answers from `src/lib/drafts.ts` and existing receipts from `submitted`, so fast input is never wiped by a passive effect (`D1`) and Back/Next navigation preserves learner work.
  * Uses `useRovingTabIndex` (`src/lib/a11y/useRovingTabIndex.ts`) for arrow-key navigation across MCQ options.
* **`src/components/scenes/index.tsx` & `src/components/scenes/stages.tsx`:**
  * Maps the 4 engine scene kinds (`binary_search`, `sorting`, `logic_gates`, `cpu_cycle`) to their pure SVG/DOM stages (`BinarySearchStage`, `SortingStage`, `LogicGatesStage`, `CpuStage`) and routes any unrecognised kind to `UnknownStage`.
  * Enforced by `src/lib/engine/player.test.mjs` (15/15 tests) and `scripts/check-contract.mjs` (verifies `backend/src/adapt/learning/sims`, `stages.tsx`, and `mock.ts` agree on the 4 kinds, and that no scene uses `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`).
